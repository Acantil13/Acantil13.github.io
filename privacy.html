Wayday privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Wayday is a desktop app for Windows. This policy explains what it does with your information. In short: everything stays on your own PC, and nobody else, including Wayday's maker, can see it.

What Wayday keeps, and where

Wayday saves your settings, your calendar events, reminders you've ticked off, your wellbeing notes, your creatures and everything else in its own folder on your PC (in your Windows user folder). It also makes daily backup copies there, and in a second folder of your choosing if you set one up. None of it is sent to Wayday's maker or to anyone else.

Google Calendar

If you choose to connect your Google account, Wayday asks Google for permission to:

  • see your calendars, so it can show your events along the path; and
  • add, change and delete events, so that events you make or change in Wayday are kept in step with Google Calendar.

Your calendar data goes only between your PC and Google. Wayday stores a copy on your PC so it works offline, uses it only to show your day and remind you, and never shares, sells or sends it anywhere else. The sign-in Google gives Wayday is stored on your PC, scrambled so it only works on that PC.

Wayday's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can disconnect at any time: sign out in Wayday's Settings (Calendar), or remove Wayday's access from your Google account's permissions page.

Weather

To show the weather, Wayday sends the town you typed in Settings (and, once it's found, that town's rough location) to Open-Meteo, a free weather service. Nothing else about you is sent.

No tracking

Wayday has no adverts, no analytics and no accounts of its own. It doesn't collect anything about how you use it.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling Wayday and deleting its folder removes everything it kept on your PC. Settings > Your data > "Open Wayday's folder" shows you where it is.

Children

Wayday isn't aimed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them; it collects nothing from anyone.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions are welcome: contact Acantil13 through GitHub.
