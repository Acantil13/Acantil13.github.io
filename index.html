Wayday

Your day as a little world.

Wayday is a cosy desktop calendar for Windows. Your day is a walk: your character strolls along a path in real time, and your appointments wait along the way as little creatures. Reminders pop up with a friendly chime, and at night a sleeper cart carries you on through the world.

What it does

  • Shows your Google Calendar along the path, and keeps it in step both ways if you connect it.
  • Reminds you before things start, and can ask whether you took your medicine or did the thing.
  • Weather for your town, seasons, festivals, creatures to befriend, a cart to make your home.
  • A morning briefing, gentle reminders to drink water and stretch, and a look back at the day.

Your privacy

Everything Wayday keeps stays on your own PC. It only talks to Google to sync your calendar (if you choose to connect it), and to a free weather service with your town's name. Read the full privacy policy.
